Refund & Support Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

We want you to buy with confidence. Here is how refunds and support work, in plain language.

The short version: if something goes wrong on our side, we fix it or refund you. Write to us within 7 days of purchase.

Digital products (guides and handbooks)

You will get a refund if:

  • you were charged but did not receive your download links;
  • the file is damaged or will not open and we cannot fix it quickly;
  • you were charged twice for the same item;
  • you received a different product from the one you paid for.

Because digital files cannot be returned once downloaded, we do not usually refund purchases made by mistake or because of a change of mind after the file has been downloaded. If you are unsure, message us before you buy and we will gladly answer your questions.

Events and masterclasses

  • If we cancel an event, you can choose a full refund or a place at the next event.
  • If we reschedule and the new date does not work for you, you can request a refund.
  • If you cannot attend, you may transfer your place to another teacher. Just tell us their name and email before the event.

How to ask for help or a refund

  1. Email hello@empowerteacher.com or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Include the email you used to pay and your Paystack payment reference (it is in your confirmation email).
  3. Tell us what went wrong. We will reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (M-Pesa or card) and can take several working days to appear, depending on your bank or mobile money provider.
