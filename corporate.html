Making a career change can feel overwhelming. You may wonder whether your qualifications are enough, how international schools hire teachers, or whether your current experience is valuable. Meet the Author

Leonard Mwendwa is an experienced international Biology educator with more than 13 years of teaching experience across international schools. As the founder of Empowered Teachers Forum, Leonard has mentored hundreds of aspiring and practicing educators, helping them build successful teaching careers both locally and internationally.


In From Corporate to Classroom, Leonard combines years of practical experience with real-world guidance to help professionals confidently transition into teaching. This book is designed to answer your biggest questions, eliminate uncertainty, and provide a clear roadmap toward a meaningful career in education.
