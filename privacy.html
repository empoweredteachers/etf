Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Empowered Teachers Forum ("we", "us") is operated by TeachLead Africa, Loresho Ridge, Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • When you join or download a free resource: your name and email address.
  • When you register for an event: your name and email address, and the event you registered for.
  • When you buy a product or paid event: your email address, your name (for events), the items purchased, the amount, and a payment reference. Payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your card number or M-Pesa PIN.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To deliver the resources, tickets and downloads you ask for.
  • To send confirmations, download links and event details.
  • To send career tips, resources and event invitations, only if you joined our list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To prevent abuse and to keep our payment and accounting records.

Who handles your data for us

  • Paystack processes payments (M-Pesa and cards).
  • Brevo stores our email list and sends our emails.
  • Meta Platforms provides the advertising measurement tool (Meta Pixel) used on our site.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site, stores our files and registration records, and helps protect against abuse.

These providers may process data outside Kenya. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Event registration records: up to 180 days after registration.
  • Download links: active for 30 days after purchase.
  • Email list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.
  • Payment and accounting records: as long as the law requires.

Cookies, advertising and third-party content

We use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure how our ads perform and to understand visits to our site. It may collect information such as the pages you view and whether you join our list, and Meta may use it under its own policies. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook and browser settings. Our pages also load Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at hello@empowerteacher.com and we will respond promptly. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
